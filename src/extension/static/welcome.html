Lock In Extended

Your coach,
in Chrome.

Tell it what you're working on. Drift to YouTube and it notices. First a nudge, then it talks, then it shouts, then it locks the site until you're done.

  1. 1

    Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Lock In Extended. The control panel lives under that icon.

  2. 2

    Lock in

    Open the panel, type the one thing you're doing, pick 25, 50 or 90 minutes. Your to-dos, goals and crew are right there too.

  3. 3

    Already use Lock In for Mac?

    Just open it. The extension links up by itself and uses the same you: profile, site labels, crew, to-dos, goals and meetings. Sessions you run in Chrome alone go into your Mac history.
